# Phase III handoff — minimum cyclic modulus

**Recommended target:** José A. R. Fonollosa, *Minimum modulus for the unique multiset-sum problem*, Conjecture 1. The target is the universal lower bound over all residue sets in finite **cyclic** groups. A satisfying set below the bound refutes it. The established upper-bound construction and the all-abelian-group theorem have separate scopes. [Original paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.08366v2)

For every (n\ge2), positive (N<2^n-2^{\lfloor\log_2 n\rfloor}), and (n)-element (A\subseteq\mathbb Z/N\mathbb Z), there exist natural multiplicities (m_a), different from the all-ones vector, with (\sum_{a\in A}m_a=n) and (\sum_{a\in A}m_aa=\sum_{a\in A}a) modulo (N). A witness for the negation consists of explicit (n,N,A) and a complete check of uniqueness across **all** size-(n) multisets over (A).

## First authorized work package

- Create a fixture list with reported provenance and expected classifications. Check a tiny reported impossible modulus such as (n=4,N=11) by complete enumeration and a known valid size-eight binary set at (N=248) by direct exact checking. Record Sitjar’s reported negative case $n=8,N=241$ and the wider exhaustive claims for $n=8,9$ as prior work. Cross-test a recursive composition enumerator and a dynamic program on multiplicity sum and residue. Count each multiset once. Export explicit nontrivial collision vectors for rejected sets.
- Use $n=10,N=1009$ as a capped diagnostic slice below the conjectured minimum 1016. The paper reports prior nonexhaustive searches through $n=13$. Check overlap before treating a result as new. Use a bounded candidate-set generator or proof-producing constraint solver. Check any symmetry reduction algebraically for the stated modulus. Translation and multiplication by a unit preserve validity. At prime moduli normalize two distinct residues to 0 and 1. At composite moduli, retain configurations without an invertible pair difference. For large instances, count and report which classes were actually generated. A solver's `UNSAT` conclusion needs an encoding argument, coverage and a checked refutation to become a certified finite result.
- Test whether collisions can be predicted from a small structural description, such as the pattern of collision vectors over subfamilies of residue sets. Record which candidate criteria survive a held-out slice. Separate the question of finding witnesses from the universal lower-bound question. Estimate growth before expanding to the next size.
- Start with at most one CPU workday for controls and a separate capped diagnostic search. Large families may need a different encoding or an improved mathematical bound. No discrete GPU is required for the checker. Defer any cloud spend and larger search until the first results are reviewed.
