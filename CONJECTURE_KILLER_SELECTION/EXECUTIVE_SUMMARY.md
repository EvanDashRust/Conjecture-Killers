# Conjecture Killer — Phase II selection

**Recommend Fonollosa’s minimum cyclic modulus conjecture for the first campaign.** A single smaller valid modulus and its explicit residue set would decide the original question. The strongest recent lead, Sitjar’s November 14 preprint, improves the lower bound and reports exhaustive cases n=8 and n=9. The unbounded cyclic question remains open on the evidence reviewed. The early work should use those cases as controls and investigate whether the collision constraints generalize. [Original problem](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.08366v2) · [Sitjar preprint](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24228247)

The first two tie at 84. Prescribed differences wins when significance has more weight. Its finite obstruction would need a trusted infeasibility certificate for an entire matching instance. Minimum modulus offers an affirmative certificate of valid-set uniqueness at one finite input and a short bridge to the published formulation. Its advantage is decisive verification and a focused entry point. Ranking uncertainty is material, especially around current independent searches.

- **Best overall — Fonollosa.** Audit the exact cyclic formulation and latest probes, cross-check known valid and excluded examples, then cap a search at n=10 and N=1009 and assess a structural collision obstruction.
- **Best fast win — Fanelli–Moscardelli.** Look for exact extremal profiles at seven and eight agents that suggest one construction for each parity. The published upper bound and separate existence question give the attempt a precise goal.
- **Best moonshot — Černý.** Compare exact shortest-word traces in the residual binary degree-two configurations identified by the November 2026 partial result. A reusable compression inequality would justify deeper work.

The board screened all 1,911 Terra statements and audited ten exact target scopes. Every score is an editorial judgment. Full-current status is limited to publicly located evidence and the stated date. Existing computational claims and Lean proofs were assessed through papers, code and reported checks. Individual certificates were outside the selection review. The Gerstenhaber and TxGraffiti claims and the recently resolved Hadamard order 668 are among the exclusions documented in the evidence file.
