# Three strategic favorites

The distinctions below reflect three different campaign objectives. The overall pick shares the highest baseline grade and wins the board’s tie-break. The fast-win pick has the most promising shape for a short constructive investigation. The moonshot pick combines a sharp, consequential conjecture with a precise unresolved structural frontier. The experiments are proposed for authorization in Phase III.

## Best overall — Fonollosa’s minimum cyclic modulus

A smaller valid modulus would be a compact, unambiguous counterexample to a recent conjecture in additive combinatorics. The question has a specific application to exact permanent extraction, a known sharp construction for one family, and Sitjar’s reported stronger cyclic lower bound and exhaustive n=8,9 cases for comparison. The bottleneck is obtaining enough equal-sum multiset collisions uniformly over arbitrary cyclic residue sets, or finding a set that avoids every such collision at a smaller modulus. Binary-coin arguments determine the behavior of the published construction. They need a new structural ingredient to address arbitrary sets. [Fonollosa, Conjecture 1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.08366v2) · [Inal’s all-abelian result](https://doi.org/10.17605/OSF.IO/C58Q9) · [Sitjar’s cyclic work](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24228247)

Use two independently written exact checkers, one for multiplicity vectors and one for a size-and-residue dynamic program with counts capped at two. Compare them on the published valid construction and on excluded small cases or documented collisions. Use $n=8,N=248$ as a positive control and $n=8,N=241$ as an exclusion replay. Compare structured cyclic sets at the prime $N=1009$ for $n=10$ under a timeout. This tests an individual point below the $n=10$ conjectured value 1016. Look for a collision obstruction that can be stated independently of a numeric cutoff. The winning output is a new candidate witness with both checkers agreeing, or an explicit structural criterion with examples that distinguish it from the known lower bounds. A timed-out or incomplete search supplies an engineering result only.

Continue if the pilot produces a valid candidate, new certified finite coverage with enough mathematical value to stand alone, or a structural reduction that applies to an infinite family. Pause if the accessible search merely repeats public coverage, if normalization silently removes possible witnesses, or if stronger lower bounds eliminate the proposed search class without suggesting another route. Cap the initial CPU run before planning broader enumeration. See the [Phase III handoff](PHASE_III_HANDOFF.md).

## Best fast win — Fanelli–Moscardelli approximate roommates threshold

The conjectured value specifies exactly how much instability a general complete roommates market must tolerate. The upper bound is in the paper. A positive resolution can therefore take a constructive form: profiles covering every sufficiently large odd and even size for which every matching has an improving pair at one step below the bound. At present, the published existence lower bound has order square root of the market size. The paper's dynamics result concerns cycles in an algorithm, while the required lower bound quantifies over all matchings. The eventual interpretation must remain visible in any write-up. [Fanelli and Moscardelli, Theorems 1–3 and §5](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/full/10.3233/FAIA251234)

Reproduce the reported small-size existence results through six agents with a direct partial-matching oracle. At seven and eight, search rank profiles with a constraint solver. Whenever a profile has a stable matching at the lower threshold, use that matching to refine the search. Check every proposed extremal profile by exhaustive matching enumeration at those sizes. A credible early success is a pair of exact witnesses with compatible odd and even patterns and an identifiable extension operation. Failure to find a witness at a particular size has no direct implication for eventual equality.

Continue if the same pattern survives enlargement while preserving the exact rank improvements, preferably with a candidate invariant showing why no matching escapes. Pause if search produces only unrelated isolated configurations or if the rank conventions diverge from the paper. Budget a few CPU days for the search and validation before deciding whether the construction warrants formalization. ## Best moonshot — Černý’s quadratic reset-word bound

This is a sharp and longstanding question about finite automata. The conjectured extremal examples are known. The mathematical bottleneck is controlling how much word length is spent reaching progressively smaller images. The November 19 Goel preprint gives a concrete opening in quasi-Eulerian degree two and describes the configurations its argument leaves out. Audit that scope and newer work before choosing which residual cases to study. [Szykuła survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24245) · [Goel, November 2026](https://hexagonmath.org/2611.00635v1)

Compare exact subset-graph breadth-first-search traces for classic extremal automata and small binary strongly connected examples in the three reported uncovered configurations. Independently check classification and witness words. Preserve failed candidate invariants and seek a single progress measure that separates hard instances from routine synchronization. Success in the pilot means finding a repeatable local compression or preimage-expansion inequality in a previously uncovered configuration, or a precisely documented obstruction that rules out applying that method there. An automaton claiming to disprove the bound needs a reset word and a certificate excluding every shorter qualifying word.

Expand if a new invariant survives varied instances and has a credible proof route beyond the sampled cases. Pause if subsequent work closes the tested class, if its definition is unstable, or if exact traces supply no distinction beyond the published arguments. Small-state work can use a CPU and subset masks. Exponential growth makes unrestricted enumeration a poor main campaign.
